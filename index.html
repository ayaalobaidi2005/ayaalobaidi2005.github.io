About me

I’m a final-year B.Sc. Cybersecurity student at Canadian University Dubai, and honestly, I can’t get enough of this field. I love the moment a pile of noisy logs turns into a story, and I love the moment a system that looked solid turns out to have a crack in it.

That’s why I’m aiming for a career in SOC analysis and penetration testing: watching for threats on the defense side, and thinking like an attacker to find weaknesses before someone else does. Ethical hacking, network security, and digital privacy are what I read about for fun.

So far I’ve built a signed-message system with RSA, written Linux tools in C, run adversarial experiments against edge services, and co-authored a research paper on the ethics and regulation of AI in cybersecurity. I’m just getting started, and I’m always learning something new. 💕
